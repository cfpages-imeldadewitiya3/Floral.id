# Article Catalog — floral.id

Scope: 96 evidence-led briefs supporting flower selection, design, ordering, handling, care, safety, responsible sourcing, and florist-network fulfillment in Indonesia. The catalog plans complete coverage; it does not authorize bulk publication.

Domain boundaries: commercial routes own live products, stock, price, service areas, terms, and conversion. Botanical coverage stops at identity useful to floristry. Gardening, medicinal use, landscape cultivation, and generic event planning are excluded. No brief may be converted into a place-name swap. `florist.co.id` is independent and may cover the same intents.

Catalog statistics: 16 parent topics; 96 briefs; six briefs per topic; 12 first-wave assets. Proposed slugs were checked against 2,077 existing sitemap locators with zero exact collisions.

Priority legend: `P0` foundational, safety-critical, or directly reduces order error; `P1` completes a high-value reader path; `P2` useful depth after hubs are stable; `P3` optional/experimental. Waves are bounded editorial groups, not a command to publish everything.
